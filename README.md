# 🛒 Zepto Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a sales analysis dashboard for Zepto,
built using a dataset containing approximately 650,000 orders.

The dashboard provides an overview of sales performance,
product category contribution, city-wise revenue, brand performance,
and order delivery status.

The objective is to transform sales data into meaningful business
insights that support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Track key sales performance indicators.
- Compare revenue across product categories.
- Identify high-performing cities.
- Analyze brand-wise revenue contribution.
- Monitor delivered, cancelled, and returned orders.
- Explore sales performance using interactive filters.

## 📈 Dashboard Components

### 1. Sales Overview
Displays total orders, total revenue, quantity sold,
average order value, and average delivery time.

### 2. Category-Wise Revenue
Compares revenue contribution across:
- Beverages
- Cleaning
- Dairy
- Frozen
- Fruits
- Personal Care
- Snacks
- Vegetables

### 3. City-Wise Revenue
Compares sales revenue across multiple cities to identify
geographical differences in sales performance.

### 4. Product Status Analysis
Visualizes the proportion of delivered, cancelled,
and returned orders.

### 5. Brand-Wise Revenue
Compares revenue contribution across brands including
Amul, Britannia, Coca-Cola, Dabur, Haldirams, Local,
Nestle, and Pepsi.

### 6. Interactive Filtering
City and product category slicers allow users to explore
the dashboard by different business segments.

## 💡 Preliminary Business Observations

- Approximately 93% of orders are delivered.
- Approximately 5% of orders are cancelled.
- Approximately 2% of orders are returned.
- Product category revenue is relatively evenly distributed
  in the displayed chart.
- Brand-level revenue contribution varies across brands.
- Revenue differs across cities, highlighting the importance
  of geographical sales analysis.

These observations are based on the dashboard's displayed values.
